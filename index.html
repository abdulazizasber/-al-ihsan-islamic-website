Life of the Prophet ﷺ

Seerah Panel

1. Birth in Makkah (570 CE)

Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was born in Makkah in the Year of the Elephant into the noble clan of Banu Hashim, known from youth as Al-Amin (The Trustworthy).

2. The First Revelation (610 CE)

In the Cave of Hira, Angel Jibril brought the first verses of Surah Al-Alaq: "Read in the name of your Lord who created."

3. The Hijrah to Madinah (622 CE)

The migration to Madinah established the first Muslim community built on brotherhood, justice, and devotion to Allah.

Fiqh Panel

Pillars of Islam

1. Shahadah (Testimony of Faith) • 2. Salah (Five Daily Prayers) • 3. Zakah (Obligatory Charity) • 4. Sawm (Fasting in Ramadan) • 5. Hajj (Pilgrimage to Makkah).

Obligatory Acts of Wudu (Fara'id)

1. Washing the entire face • 2. Washing both arms including the elbows • 3. Wiping the head • 4. Washing both feet including the ankles.
