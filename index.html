Daily Hadith

Intentions

إِنَّمَا الْأَعْمَالُ بِالنِّيَّاتِ

“Actions are judged by intentions, and every person will have what they intended.”

Sahih al-Bukhari 1; Sahih Muslim 1907.

Quran

A reminder of ease

فَإِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا ۝ إِنَّ مَعَ الْعُسْرِ يُسْرًا

Surely with hardship comes ease. Surely with hardship comes ease.

Quran 94:5–6. Translation wording may vary.

Keep turning back to Allah

Duas

Good in this life and the next

رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

Our Lord, grant us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.

Quran 2:201
